Most AI projects that stall have something in common: they started with a technology and went looking for a problem.

## The one question

Before choosing a model, a vendor or a framework, ask:

**"What would we do differently on Monday if this worked?"**

If nobody can answer that clearly, the project isn't ready.

## A simple way to find good problems

1. Talk to the people closest to the work. Ask where they lose time.
2. Look for tasks that are repetitive, text-heavy, or rely on judgment that can be written down.
3. Pick the smallest version you could test in two weeks.

Small, real wins build the confidence (and the budget) for bigger ones.
